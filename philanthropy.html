What HelpOne does

What HelpOne does for a nonprofit

Most nonprofits run on spreadsheets, sticky notes, and a few tired people. HelpOne puts volunteers, staff, events, fundraising, and grants in one place, on any phone, with no app to download.

Everyone on the team can use it, from the board to the newest volunteer. Less time on paperwork, more time on the mission.

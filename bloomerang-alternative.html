What you get in HelpOne

  • Donors & contacts. One record per person, with giving and volunteer hours together. Merge duplicates in one click. Groups sync to Mailchimp.
  • Fundraising. Gifts, pledges, auctions, sponsorships and giving statements.
  • Volunteers. Online sign-up with waivers, shifts, waitlists, self check-in and approved hours.
  • Events. Free RSVPs with QR codes. Tickets and gifts land in each person's history. Event P&L.
  • Finances. GAAP-ready nonprofit financial statements under ASC 958 (financial position, activities by donor restriction, functional expenses and cash flows), with fund accounting, accruals and a locked, audited ledger. Set your own fiscal year. Export to Excel or typeset PDF statements. Form 990 and CHAR500 are auto-filled. Plus fund balances, Schedule B and budget vs actual.
  • Board. A board portal, votes, conflict-of-interest forms, terms and a skills matrix.
  • HR & training. Onboarding documents with e-sign, reviews, time off, timesheets and training records.
  • Operations. Equipment and software tracking, renewal reminders and purchase orders.

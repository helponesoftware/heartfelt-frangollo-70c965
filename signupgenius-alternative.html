Who each one is best for

Choose HelpOne if…

You run an ongoing volunteer program and want sign-up, shifts, check-in, reminders, background checks and approved hours in one place, next to your donors and events.

Choose SignUpGenius if…

You need a quick, one-off sign-up sheet for a single event, and you don’t need a record of each volunteer over time.

What volunteers and coordinators get

Smart availability

Moving your volunteers over

  1. Gather your list. Pull together the volunteers you already have from your sign-up sheets.
  2. Start your HelpOne trial. Send your volunteers your online sign-up link, or ask us to help bring your list in.
  3. Add your shifts. Set the skills each shift needs and turn on waitlists and reminders.
  4. Share the portal. Volunteers claim shifts, check in and see their own hours.

Questions

Is HelpOne free like a basic sign-up sheet?

No. HelpOne is $299 a month or $2,990 a year with unlimited volunteers and every module, including donors, events, HR and finances. If you only need one sign-up sheet, a free sign-up tool may be enough.

Can volunteers sign up without staff help?

Yes. They use your online sign-up form, then claim open shifts, check in and see their hours in the volunteer portal.

Does HelpOne send shift reminders?

Yes, by text and email, with consent, STOP handling and quiet hours. 1,000 texts a month are included (fair use).

Can I count volunteer hours for my 990?

Yes. Approved hours are valued at the rate you set and are ready for Form 990, and volunteers can download signed hours letters.

Is there a limit on volunteers?

No. Unlimited volunteers, users and events.

Can I try HelpOne first?

Yes. 14 days, every module, no card. When the trial ends, your data stays saved and read-only until you pick a plan.

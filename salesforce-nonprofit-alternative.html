Where Salesforce is stronger

  • Configurable. You can shape Salesforce to unusual programs and processes, and build on it with partners and apps.
  • Programs and grants. Program Management and Outcomes and Grantmaking are listed on the pricing page.
  • 10 free licenses. Through Power of Us, eligible nonprofits get 10 Nonprofit Cloud Core licenses at no cost.
  • Scale and community. Salesforce says over 60,000 nonprofits use it, and there is a large community of users and partners.

If you need a custom-built system or heavy program tracking, Salesforce is a strong choice.

Where HelpOne is different

  • Ready to use. Volunteers, donors, events, HR, board and finance are already built and share one login and one record per person.
  • One price, unlimited users. $299 a month or $2,990 a year. No per-user fees and no annual contract.
  • Nonprofit finances. Financial statements with fund accounting, Excel and PDF export, and an auto-filled Form 990 and CHAR500. It doesn't replace your CPA.
  • Easy to leave. No contracts. Cancel anytime in two clicks, and take your data with you.

Typical cost of separate tools for volunteers, HR, e-sign, events and a donor database: about $1,500 a month on average (our estimate; yours will vary).

Questions

Is HelpOne cheaper than Salesforce for nonprofits?

It depends on how many people need a login. Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud Core starts at $70 per user per month, billed annually, and eligible nonprofits get 10 Core licenses at no cost through Power of Us. For example, at the listed price, 15 staff would be 10 free licenses plus 5 at $70, or $350 a month. HelpOne is $299 a month with unlimited users and every module. Salesforce may also need setup help, and HR, board or accounting tools are not listed on its pricing page. Check both pages for your numbers.

What is NPSP, and is it going away?

NPSP is the Nonprofit Success Pack, a nonprofit data model for fundraising and program management built on the Salesforce platform. Salesforce says NPSP will continue to be supported. Nonprofit Cloud is the newer Salesforce nonprofit product. You don't have to move off NPSP because of this page; the choice is yours.

Does HelpOne do everything Salesforce does?

No. Salesforce is a configurable platform with a large partner network, and it lists tools such as program outcomes and a grantmaking suite. HelpOne is ready to use as it comes. It covers volunteers, donors, events, HR, finances and your board, but it is not a build-it-yourself platform.

Can I move my data from Salesforce to HelpOne?

Yes. Export your contacts and gifts from Salesforce as reports or CSV files, start the free trial, and import them. Migration help is available. NYCON members get free migration.

Is there a contract?

Not with HelpOne. No contracts. Cancel anytime in two clicks, and take your data with you. Salesforce says its Nonprofit Cloud editions require an annual contract.

Can I try HelpOne first?

Yes. 14 days, every module, no card. When the trial ends, your data stays saved and read-only until you pick a plan.

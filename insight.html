The period you need

Month by month, next to the same months last year.

  • Choose Week, Month, Quarter, year to date, fiscal year to date, or the last 30 days.
  • A trend line shows how the period is building, against the prior period. Side-by-side bars put each month next to the same month last year.
  • Fiscal year to date follows the fiscal year you already set in HelpOne.

Where the money went

Sources, how people paid, and funds.

  • See where the money came from — gifts, sponsorships, and the rest of your mix.
  • See how people paid: card, bank transfer, check, or pledge.
  • Giving by fund, with each fund next to the same dates last year.

Donors

Your top donors, and who's new.

  • Top donors for the period, ranked by what they gave.
  • New versus returning donors, so you can see who came back and who is giving for the first time.

Volunteers

Hours against last year, with the dollar value already worked out.

  • Volunteer hours month by month, next to the same months last year.
  • An estimated dollar value, at the hourly rate you set.
  • How many shift spots are filled for the next 30 days.

Events

Every event side by side.

  • Tickets, gifts and sponsorships, stacked in one bar per event.
  • A single total for what each event raised, so you can compare them at a glance.

The board pack

One click. Charts on top, the table underneath.

  • Open the board monthly pack from Reports. Giving, people, volunteers and events in one snapshot.
  • The same graphs you just reviewed sit above a table the board can read.
  • Export a spreadsheet or a PDF when you're ready to send it.

People only see what they should. Gift dollars stay with the people who need them.

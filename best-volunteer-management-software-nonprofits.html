The short answer

  • Only need volunteers? A dedicated tool usually costs less. Track it Forward lists plans from $13.25/month, Bloomerang Volunteer starts at $119/month and VolunteerHub from $143/month.
  • Already use a donor CRM? Look at that company's volunteer add-on first, such as Bloomerang Volunteer or the Neon CRM Volunteers module, so volunteer and donor records stay together.
  • Large or multi-location program? Look closely at Galaxy Digital and Better Impact. Both list features for multi-location programs, and both are quote-only.
  • Volunteers are one part of a bigger job? If you also run donors, events, staff, a board and the books, HelpOne puts them in one login at $299/month, with unlimited volunteers.

What to look for

Easy sign-up for volunteers

If signing up is hard, people don't. Look for online sign-up, a portal where volunteers see open shifts, and a way to find opportunities from their phone.

Scheduling that matches how you work

One-time and recurring shifts, roles, waitlists when a shift is full, and reminders so people show up.

Waivers and onboarding

Digital waivers and forms, so you collect paperwork once and don't chase it on paper.

Hours you can trust

Check-in and check-out, hours approval, and reports you can give to funders, your board and your auditor.

One record per person

A volunteer is often also a donor or an event guest. If the tool keeps them in separate databases, you will merge lists by hand.

A price you can plan around

Ask what sets the price: volunteer count, number of admins, modules or revenue. Ask what it costs at your size and in three years.

A checklist to use on every demo

  • ☐How many volunteers do we have now, and how many in three years?
  • ☐What sets the price: volunteer count, admins, plan tier or modules? What does it cost at our size?
  • ☐Which of these are included, and which cost extra: text reminders, waivers, kiosk check-in, reports, an app?
  • ☐How many staff can be administrators? Can team leaders see their own groups?
  • ☐Can volunteers sign up, see shifts and log hours without calling us?
  • ☐Can we import our spreadsheet or old system, and is migration help free or paid?
  • ☐Can we export everything, any time, and cancel without a fee? Is there a contract?
  • ☐Does volunteer data connect to our donor records, or do we copy it by hand?
  • ☐What support do we get, and when? Chat, email, phone, hours?
  • ☐Who on our team will own it day to day?

Ask every company the same ten questions and write the answers down. It makes the choice much easier.

Six options, from their own pages

Bloomerang Volunteer

A volunteer product that connects to Bloomerang CRM

  • Starts at $119/month, billed annually.
  • Lists volunteer management, scheduling with waitlists, digital waivers and e-signatures, email and SMS messages, hours and attendance tracking, and a mobile app for volunteers and managers.
  • Syncs volunteer and donor data with Bloomerang CRM, which is a separate product that starts at $125/month.

Source: bloomerang.com/pricing

Neon CRM Volunteers module

An add-on module inside Neon CRM

  • Neon CRM starts at $99/month, based on your revenue. The Volunteers module adds 10% of your CRM fee.
  • Lists volunteer roles, shift management, timesheets, communications, self-service and dashboards.
  • Volunteers sit in the same profile as donors, events and gifts.

Source: neonone.com/solutions/neon-crm, neonone.com/pricing

Galaxy Digital (Get Connected)

Volunteer software for programs of many sizes, including volunteer centers

  • Price not published. Each product is custom-priced, so you need a quote. It says it never charges by the number of volunteers.
  • Lists shift and recurring scheduling, groups and teams, email and text messages, shift reminders, digital waivers and consent forms, awards, and 50+ reports. Custom reports are an optional add-on.
  • Has an open API and lists a Salesforce integration. It also describes a free interface that connects you to a local Volunteer Center.

Source: galaxydigital.com/get-connected-faq

VolunteerHub

Volunteer registration and scheduling, with plan tiers

  • Plus starts at $143/month and Pro at $288/month, both billed annually plus a set-up fee. Enterprise is by quote.
  • Lists volunteer self-scheduling, landing pages for each opportunity, waitlists, hours tracking, waivers, a mobile app for check-in and check-out, a kiosk, custom reports and duplicate merge. Check which plan includes each one.

Source: volunteerhub.com/pricing

Better Impact (Volunteer Impact)

Volunteer software from Better Impact, in three plans

  • Price not published on its page; you book a call for a quote. It says pricing depends on your plan and your number of accepted and inactive volunteer profiles.
  • Foundation lists online applications, scheduling, hours tracking and branded forms. Growth adds online training, automated reminders and e-signing. Impact adds multi-location oversight and location-specific permissions.

Source: betterimpact.com/pricing

Track it Forward

A simple volunteer hours and events tracker

  • Plans listed at $13.25, $25.00 and $37.00 a month. The price depends on how many active volunteers you have. A free plan is listed for organizations with fewer than 25 volunteers, and a 30-day trial needs no card.
  • The lowest plan lists time tracking, an event calendar, reports, a mobile app, milestones and email. Higher plans add hours approval, more than one administrator, custom fields, a digital sign-in sheet, text messages and a check-in kiosk.

Source: trackitforward.com/pricing

Also worth a look: Virtuous Volunteer, which has personalized pricing. See the Virtuous alternative page. Plans change; always check each company's page for what's included in each plan.

Where HelpOne fits

HelpOne is a good fit if…

Volunteers are one part of a bigger job. You also run donors, events, staff, a board and the books, and you would rather have one login, one price and one record per person. HelpOne has a volunteer self-service portal with availability, open shifts, timesheets and documents, online sign-up with waivers, shifts and waitlists, self check-in, an approval workflow for hours, hours valued for Form 990, and a master dashboard. Unlimited volunteers are included.

Look elsewhere if…

You only need volunteer scheduling and hours, and want the lowest monthly price. Track it Forward lists plans from $13.25/month, and several tools above start well under $299. Also look elsewhere if you run a large multi-location volunteer program: Better Impact lists location-specific permissions on its top plan and Galaxy Digital lists multi-location features and a Volunteer Center product.

If you pay for separate tools for volunteers, donors, events, HR, board and accounting, the total is about $1,500 a month on average (our estimate; yours will differ). Add up your own list and compare it with $299/month. See the HelpOne volunteer management page for more, the donor software buyer's guide, or the full comparison.

How to decide, step by step

  1. Write down what you need: how many volunteers, how many admins, what you do today on paper.
  2. Cut the list to two or three tools using the checklist above.
  3. Ask each for a quote for your size, in writing, with what's included.
  4. Try a free trial or demo with a real shift. HelpOne has a 14-day free trial, no card.
  5. Check that you can export your data and cancel without a fee before you sign.

Questions

What is volunteer management software?

It is software that helps a nonprofit sign up volunteers, schedule shifts, track hours and keep in touch. Some tools do only that. Others are part of a larger system that also holds donors, events and finances.

How much does volunteer management software cost?

It varies a lot. As of Oct 2026, Track it Forward lists plans from $13.25/month and a free plan for fewer than 25 volunteers. Bloomerang Volunteer starts at $119/month billed annually, and VolunteerHub Plus starts at $143/month billed annually plus a set-up fee. Neon CRM adds 10% of your CRM fee for its Volunteers module. Galaxy Digital and Better Impact don't publish prices on the pages we checked, so you need a quote. HelpOne is $299/month or $2,990/year with every module. Check each company's site before you decide.

Do I need a separate volunteer tool or an all-in-one?

It depends on what else you run. If volunteers are your only need, a dedicated volunteer tool can cost less. If you also need donor records, events, HR, a board portal or finances, an all-in-one such as HelpOne keeps volunteers and donors in one record per person and one price.

What features matter most?

For most small nonprofits: online sign-up, shift scheduling with waitlists, digital waivers, check-in, hours tracking with approval, reminders, reports for funders, and the ability to export your data. Use the checklist on this page on every demo.

Is HelpOne a good fit if I only need volunteer scheduling?

Maybe not. HelpOne starts at $299/month and includes much more than volunteers. If you only need volunteer scheduling and hours, a dedicated tool with a lower published price may cost less. HelpOne fits best when volunteers are one part of a bigger job.

Can I try HelpOne first?

Can I move my volunteer list to HelpOne?

Yes. Export your volunteer list from your current tool or spreadsheet, start the free trial and import it. Migration help is available. NYCON members get free migration.

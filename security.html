Certified today vs. how it works

We separate formal certifications from architecture choices so you can diligence us without guessing.

Topic Status today What that means
SOC 2 Type II Certified Independent audit of our security controls over time—not just a point-in-time checklist.
PCI DSS Level 1 Via payment processor Card payments run through our PCI DSS Level 1 processor partner (NMI). HelpOne is not designed to store full card numbers (PAN) on our systems.
AES-256 encryption In practice Data is protected with modern encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest using industry-standard AES-256 approaches.
GDPR Aligned practices We follow GDPR-aligned data handling practices (access, retention, deletion requests). This is not a separate “GDPR certificate.”
HIPAA Not a HIPAA claim HelpOne can support nonprofits that handle sensitive operational data, but we do not market a blanket HIPAA certification on this page. Ask us if you need a BAA discussion for your use case.

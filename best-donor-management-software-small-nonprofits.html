The short answer

There is no single best tool. The right one depends on how big your list is, who will run it, and whether donors are your only job. Here is where each kind of option tends to fit.

Only need a donor database, lowest price

Little Green Light starts at $45/month for up to 2,500 records, with unlimited users. It is a donor database, so you will use other tools for the rest.

Donor CRM with fundraising depth

Bloomerang, Neon CRM and Virtuous are built around fundraising. Each lists tools such as online giving, email and reporting. Ask each for a quote at your size.

You have an admin or a consultant

Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud can be shaped to almost any process. Eligible nonprofits get 10 free Core licenses. Plan time or a partner for setup and upkeep.

Email tools built in

DonorPerfect lists Constant Contact email built in, donation forms, volunteer tracking and grant tools, in Core, Plus and Pro tiers. Pricing is by quote.

One login for the whole organization

HelpOne covers donors, volunteers, events, HR, finances and your board for $299/month, with unlimited users and contacts. It is not a specialist fundraising CRM.

Not sure yet

Use the checklist below on two or three demos. Price your own size, not the cheapest plan on the page.

What to look for

What sets the price

Some tools charge by record count, some by revenue, some by user, some by module. Pick the one that stays fair as you grow. Little Green Light charges by records, Neon CRM by revenue, Salesforce by user.

What is included

Donor records and gifts are the basics. Check whether volunteers, events, memberships, email and reports are included or sold as add-ons.

Who can use it

Per-user pricing adds up as staff, board members and volunteers need access. Several tools list unlimited users. Check, and ask what “user” means.

How hard it is to run

Ask who sets it up, who keeps it clean, and how long training takes. A powerful tool nobody has time to maintain does not help a small team.

Your data, your exit

You should be able to export every record and cancel without a penalty. Ask about contract length and cancellation before you sign.

Support and migration

Look at support hours and channels, and whether help moving your old data is free, paid or self-serve.

A checklist to use on every demo

  • ☐How many records do we have today, and how many in three years?
  • ☐What sets the price: records, revenue, users or modules? What does it cost at our size?
  • ☐Which of these are included, and which cost extra: online giving forms, events, volunteers, memberships, email, reports?
  • ☐How many people on our team can use it? Are staff, board and volunteers all covered?
  • ☐Who pays payment processing fees, how much are they, and are donations deposited straight to our account?
  • ☐Can we import our spreadsheet or old system, and is migration help free or paid?
  • ☐Can we export everything, any time, and cancel without a fee? Is there a contract?
  • ☐What support do we get, and when? Chat, email, phone, hours?
  • ☐Does it connect to our accounting? Can it produce what our board and funders ask for?
  • ☐Who on our team will own it day to day?

Ask every company the same ten questions and write the answers down. It makes the choice much easier.

Questions

What is donor management software?

It is a database for the people who support your nonprofit. It keeps each person's contact details, gifts, pledges, notes and communication history in one record, so your team can thank donors, send receipts and see who gives and how often. Some tools stop there. Others add online giving forms, events, volunteers and accounting.

What is the cheapest donor management software for a small nonprofit?

On published prices, Little Green Light starts lowest: $45/month for up to 2,500 constituent records (pricing as of Oct 2026, on its own pricing page). It is a donor database, so volunteers, events, HR and accounting would need other tools. Salesforce gives eligible nonprofits 10 free Nonprofit Cloud Core licenses through its Power of Us program, but setup and upkeep take more work.

How much should a small nonprofit pay for donor software?

Published starting prices for donor databases run from about $45 to $125 a month for a small list. The total grows with record count, revenue, users or modules, depending on the company. Add anything you buy separately, such as volunteer, event, accounting and board tools, and the monthly total is often much higher. Check each company's pricing page for your size.

Do I need a donor CRM, or an all-in-one system?

If fundraising is your main job and you already have good tools for everything else, a donor CRM may be all you need. If you also run volunteers, events, staff, a board and the books, an all-in-one system can mean fewer logins, no duplicate records and one bill. HelpOne is built for the second case.

Is HelpOne the best choice for every small nonprofit?

No. If you only need a donor database and want the lowest price, Little Green Light starts lower. If you want a donor CRM with deep fundraising tools such as peer-to-peer campaigns or predictive insights, look closely at Bloomerang, Neon CRM or Virtuous. HelpOne fits best when you want donors, volunteers, events, HR, finances and your board in one login at one price.

What does HelpOne cost?

$299/month or $2,990/year, with unlimited users, volunteers, contacts, donors and events. There is a 14-day free trial, no card needed, and no contracts.

Can I move my donor data from another system?

Yes. Export your contacts and gifts as a spreadsheet from your current system, start the free trial and import them. Migration help is available. NYCON members get free migration.

How do I compare prices fairly?

Ask what sets the price (records, revenue, users or modules), what is an add-on, whether there is a contract, and who charges payment processing fees. Then price your own size, not the cheapest plan on the page.

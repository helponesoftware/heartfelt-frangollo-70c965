HelpOne under your name

For associations, networks and consultants who look after many nonprofits. Give your members one system for volunteers, donors, events, finances, HR, board and training, under your name.

Who it's for

  • Associations and councils that serve member nonprofits
  • Networks and federations with local chapters
  • Consultants and accountants who set up and support nonprofits

What you get

  • Every HelpOne module for each organization you bring on, with each one's data kept separate
  • Email sent from your own address and name
  • Texts sent from your own number
  • Your logo on receipts, certificates, invoices and every PDF

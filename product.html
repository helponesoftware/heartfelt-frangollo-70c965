Volunteers

“I spend my week texting volunteers and fixing the spreadsheet.”

  • Volunteers sign up online in three steps: emergency contact, a guardian OK for minors, availability, a waiver and your own questions.
  • They find open shifts, claim them and check in themselves, with a QR card or a printable kiosk.
  • Each shift lists the skills it needs and shows which volunteers have them. Waitlists fill open spots on their own.
  • Approve hours or send them back with a reason. Only approved hours count toward your 990.
Volunteer Management →

People & HR

“Every new hire means chasing paperwork and signatures.”

  • Mark the documents a new hire must sign. HelpOne sends one email, one reminder, then every signed copy.
  • E-sign is built in, with a stamped PDF and an audit page.
  • Manager reviews follow the self review, and every review downloads as a PDF.
  • Everyone sees their own time-off balance. Only HR, a person's manager and the person can see pay.
  • Fair-chance hiring steps are built in.
HR Solutions →Policies & Procedures →

Timesheets & expenses

“Timesheets and receipts come in by email, and someone has to chase every one.”

  • Timesheets are created for you each period.
  • Pick an event or in-house work, and labor cost rolls up under the event.
  • Timesheets, expenses and time off wait in approval queues on your dashboard.
  • Every expense decision records who decided, and why it was sent back.
  • Approved timesheets go to the payroll contact you name.
My HelpOne →

Training

“Nobody knows who's finished which training until someone asks.”

  • See who has finished each training and who is still due.
  • Upload a completion certificate, PDF or photo, for each person.
  • Supervisors are linked to real staff records and show on reports and exports.
  • Trainings show on the calendar, and overdue ones are flagged.
Training Tracking →

Calendar

“Events, trainings and deadlines live on five different calendars.”

  • One calendar with layers you switch on and off: events, trainings, background checks, filing deadlines and holidays.
  • Click an entry to jump to the volunteer, the staff file or the training.
  • Overdue and expired items are labelled plainly.
  • Holidays repeat, and next year's dates are one click away.
My HelpOne →

Donors & contacts

“The same person is in three systems, and none of them has the full story.”

  • One person, one record: volunteers, staff and board members link to a single contact, with their giving and hours together.
  • Merge duplicates with one button. A contact with giving history can't be deleted.
  • Notes and a clear next follow-up date on every contact.
  • An engagement badge on each contact shows who keeps coming back.
  • Your groups sync to Mailchimp as tags.
Donors & Contacts →Participants →

Events

“Registration is in one tool, money in another, and the guest list in a spreadsheet.”

  • Free RSVPs on one screen, with an instant confirmation, a QR code, an add-to-calendar link and a share link.
  • A short link and QR code your staff can hand out.
  • Every ticket, gift and auction win lands in the person's giving history.
  • Event P&L counts volunteer time as estimated value, not a cost.
Event Management →

Fundraising & finances

“Year-end reports mean a week of digging through spreadsheets.”

  • Gifts, pledges, auctions and sponsorships in one place. Company sponsors credit the company.
  • Reports for gifts with benefits, Schedule B, Schedule G Part II, fund balances, deposits and giving statements.
  • Export any area to Excel, or export everything at once.
  • Filing deadlines are tagged Overdue or Due in N days.
Finances →Fundraising →

Grants

“The grant report is due and the timesheets aren't signed.”

  • Weekly grant timesheets, signed by the employee and approved by the supervisor.
  • The supervisor gets an email when a sheet is signed.
  • Budget vs actual and staff allocation in grids you can export to Excel.
  • The functional export splits salaries by allocation.
Operations →

Board

“Board packets go out by email, and nobody can find last month's vote.”

  • A board portal with meetings, documents, votes, tasks and each member's profile.
  • Each member sees only their own committees' items and tasks.
  • One-time votes with comments, and a 3-step conflict-of-interest form.
  • Terms, a skills matrix, and tasks with steps and signed updates.
Board Management →

IT & software

“Nobody knows which laptops we own or when the subscriptions renew.”

  • Track equipment and who has it, with its condition recorded when it's handed out.
  • Track software subscriptions with their monthly or yearly cost and the seats handed out.
  • Renewal reminders by email.
  • Purchase orders with the order attached.
IT & Operations →

Access & settings

“Either everyone sees everything, or nobody can find anything.”

  • Groups and privileges, plus “Who has this” to see who holds each one.
  • “Preview as” shows the app the way any group sees it. “Hide in menu” tidies a menu without taking access away.
  • A view-only accountant login that sees Finances and nothing else.
  • Edit every dropdown list in one place.
  • A Set up HelpOne checklist: billing, access, connections, import, staff, finances and logo.

Connections

“Background checks, email lists, texts and payments each need their own login.”

  • Checkr background checks for employees, candidates and volunteers. You sign up with Checkr and pay Checkr directly.
  • Check completion and re-check dates feed the calendar.
  • Mailchimp: your HelpOne groups sync as tags.
  • Texts for shift reminders and notices, with consent, STOP handling and quiet hours. 1,000 texts a month included (fair use).
  • Take payments through your own NMI account.
Payments →

Ask HelpOne & the Daily brief

“You need one number, and it takes twenty minutes to find.”

  • Ask HelpOne answers plain questions about your own organization: who's due, what's pending, upcoming shifts, top gifts.
  • It's read-only, and it only sees what your login can see.
  • The Daily brief puts today's to-dos on your dashboard, with an optional 7 AM email.
  • One search box finds contacts, volunteers, staff, events, board members and gifts.

  • One photo is shared across all of a person's records.
  • Photos show in lists, schedules and approvals.

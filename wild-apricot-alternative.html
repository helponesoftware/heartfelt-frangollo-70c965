A Wild Apricot alternative
that runs your whole nonprofit.

Wild Apricot is membership software that a lot of clubs and associations use. HelpOne puts membership next to everything else you run: volunteers, donors, events, HR, finances and your board, from one login, at one price.

HelpOne is $299/mo or $2,990/yr with every module and no contact limits. Wild Apricot is priced by how many contacts you have. See HelpOne Memberships.

Who each one is best for

Choose HelpOne if…

You want members, donors, volunteers, events and the books in one place, and you don’t want your price to go up as your list grows. Lapsed members stay on file without moving you to a higher tier.

Choose Wild Apricot if…

You mostly need membership and a website builder in one tool, your contact list is small, and you use its own payment processing.

What HelpOne Memberships does

Everything below is live in HelpOne today, and it’s part of the one plan. See the full Memberships page.

Levels that fit how you work

Individual, household, student, sustaining. Renew on the join date or a set date like January 1. Calendar-year levels can charge only for the months left.

Renewals that go out on their own

Reminders 30 days before, 7 days before, on the due day and 7 days after. Change the days any time. Each one goes once.

One click to renew, no password

Every reminder has the member’s private renew link. After the due date they get a grace period you set.

Dues in the books

Every dues payment posts as Membership Dues. Record checks, cash and part payments against any invoice.

A directory members control

Members choose to be listed and what shows. Only current members can see it and search it.

Member prices on events

Set a member price on any ticket. Current members get it at checkout with the email on their membership. No codes to hand out.

Moving from Wild Apricot

  1. Export from Wild Apricot. Go to Contacts, then Export. Pick CSV and all fields.
  2. Start your HelpOne trial. Open Membership Services, go to Import & export and choose the file.
  3. Check the preview. HelpOne shows how many members are new, already here or skipped before anything changes.
  4. Import. Levels you don’t have yet are added for you. Run the same file twice and it updates, not doubles.

Questions

Is HelpOne cheaper than Wild Apricot?

It depends on your size. Wild Apricot starts at $66 a month for 100 contacts and goes up as your contact count grows. HelpOne is $299 a month or $2,990 a year at any size, and it also covers volunteers, donors, events, finances, HR and board. If you pay for other tools too, HelpOne usually costs less overall.

Can I import my members from Wild Apricot?

Yes. Export your contacts from Wild Apricot as a CSV and import it under Membership Services. HelpOne reads the User ID, name, email, phone, organization, city, level, status, member since and renewal due columns, and maps Wild Apricot statuses to HelpOne’s.

Do lapsed members cost extra?

No. HelpOne has no per-record fees. Lapsed members stay on file and you can still reach them.

Does HelpOne have a member directory?

Yes. Members choose whether to be listed and whether their city, email and phone show. Only current members can see it.

Is Memberships an extra cost?

No. Memberships is part of the one HelpOne plan with every other module.

Can I try HelpOne first?

Yes. 14 days, every module, no card. When the trial ends, your data stays saved and read-only until you pick a plan.

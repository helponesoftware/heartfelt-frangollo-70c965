Common Questions

Does HelpOne replace my accounting software?

No. HelpOne Finances covers the nonprofit side: fund balances, Schedule B, giving statements and budget vs actual. Every area exports to Excel for your accountant.

Does HelpOne include payroll?

No. Keep your payroll provider. Approved timesheets go to the payroll contact you name.

Does HelpOne replace Google Workspace or Microsoft 365?

No. Your team continues using Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 for email and calendar. Your email and calendar stay where they are. Documents you keep in HelpOne are stored securely.

Do I need technical staff to implement HelpOne?

No. The Set up HelpOne checklist walks you through it, and every module has a help article.

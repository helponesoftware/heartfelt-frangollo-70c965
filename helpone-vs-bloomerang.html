Where Bloomerang is stronger

  • Bloomerang has been a donor CRM for much longer than HelpOne has existed. Its retention and donor-insight tools go deeper.
  • The Giving Platform includes predictive donor insights and Prospect AI.
  • It has mobile apps for fundraisers, donors and volunteers.

If deep donor analytics is the main thing you need, Bloomerang is a strong choice.

Where HelpOne is different

  • One price. $299 a month or $2,990 a year covers every module. It doesn't change as your list grows.
  • The whole organization. Volunteers, donors, events, HR, training, policies, board and finance share one login and one record per person.
  • Real nonprofit accounting. GAAP-ready statements under ASC 958, fund accounting, accruals and a locked, audited ledger, with Excel and PDF export. It doesn't replace your CPA.
  • Form 990 and CHAR500. Approved volunteer hours and your finance data flow into an auto-filled Form 990 and CHAR500.
  • Easy to leave. No contracts. Cancel anytime in two clicks, and take your data with you.

Questions

Is HelpOne cheaper than Bloomerang?

It depends on what you need. Bloomerang's CRM starts at $125/month, billed annually, with Fundraising from $40/month (sold with the CRM) and Volunteer from $119/month, and its pricing is constituent-based. Pricing as of Oct 2026, check their site. HelpOne is $299/month or $2,990/year for every module, at any size. If you also pay for HR, board, e-sign or accounting tools, HelpOne usually costs less overall. Check both pricing pages for your size.

Does HelpOne do everything Bloomerang does for donors?

Not everything. HelpOne covers donor records, giving history, pledges, giving statements, duplicate merge and Mailchimp sync. Bloomerang's donor analytics, such as predictive giving insights and Prospect AI, go further.

Does HelpOne have accounting?

Yes. HelpOne produces GAAP-ready nonprofit financial statements under ASC 958: financial position, activities by donor restriction, functional expenses, and cash flows, with fund accounting, accruals, and a locked, audited ledger, exportable to Excel and PDF. Bloomerang's pricing page doesn't list accounting.

Is there a contract?

Not with HelpOne. No contracts. Cancel anytime in two clicks, and take your data with you. Bloomerang's published prices are billed annually.

Can I try HelpOne first?

Yes. 14 days, every module, no card. When the trial ends, your data stays saved and read-only until you pick a plan.

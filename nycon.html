Sound familiar?

Most nonprofits we talk to are doing good work with tools that don't talk to each other.

  • The spreadsheet holds it all together.Volunteer hours, donor lists and sign-ups live in files only one person really understands.
  • Five logins for one job.Sign-ups in one tool, donors in another, e-sign and email in a third, and you copy between them at night.
  • Board reports take a weekend.Every meeting means pulling numbers from different places and building the packet by hand.
  • 990 season means digging.Hours, gifts and restricted funds have to be found and added up before anyone can file.

Questions

Do you move our data for us?

Yes. Migration is free for NYCON members. Send us your spreadsheets or an export from your current tools, and we'll help you map it into HelpOne. You don't have to switch everything at once; many organizations start with the area causing the most friction.

How does the free trial work?

14 days with every module, and no card needed. When the trial ends, everything is saved and read-only until you choose a plan. Mention NYCON when you start to get the member rate.

What if it's not a fit?

No contracts. Cancel anytime in two clicks, and take your data with you.

Most organizations are up and running within a few weeks.

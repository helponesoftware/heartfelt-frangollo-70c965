Committees

Chairs, seats, and members in one place.

Create committees, set cadence and authorized seats, assign chairs and members, and see upcoming meetings and open tasks without leaving the record.

  • Active / inactive filters with search and sort
  • Members tab with roles and term end dates
  • Linked meetings, documents, and tasks

Meetings

Agendas, attendees, and follow-through.

Schedule board and committee meetings, build agenda order with presenters and duration, track attendance, and keep documents and minutes attached to the meeting.

  • Upcoming / past / draft filters
  • Location or video link on every meeting
  • Open actions counted on the meeting summary

Documents

Packets with versions and approvals.

Store governance documents tied to committees and meetings. Upload versions, request approvals, manage access groups, and track review dates—without a separate file dump.

  • Recent / Board / Committee filters
  • Draft, under review, and approved statuses
  • Version history with uploaders and dates

Votes

Motions with quorum and clear results.

Run approval votes linked to the right committee and meeting. Set open/close windows, thresholds, eligible voters, and see yes / no / abstain with cast vs pending status—including portal casts.

  • Open / scheduled / closed filters
  • Participation rates and closing dates
  • Results, voters, documents, and activity tabs

Compliance

COI disclosures you can actually audit.

Track conflict-of-interest disclosures per board member—submitted date, expiration (+1 year), reviewed-by, questionnaire answers, and signature details. Filter Current, Expired, or Missing in one glance.

  • Status badges on the member list
  • Disclosure answers with edit history
  • Mark reviewed / add disclosure workflows

Tasks

Action items owned after the meeting.

Assign board work with priority, due dates, and status. Link tasks to committees and meetings so follow-ups do not vanish into email threads.

  • Open / overdue / completed filters
  • Assign to board members with priority levels
  • Related committee and meeting fields

External Board Portal

A simpler menu for board members.

Admins get the full Board suite. Portal users get a focused member experience—so directors can find meetings, packets, votes, and their own tasks without the admin complexity.

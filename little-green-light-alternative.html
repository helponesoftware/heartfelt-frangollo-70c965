A Little Green Light alternative
that runs your whole nonprofit.

Little Green Light is a simple, low-cost donor database, and many small nonprofits like it. HelpOne does a bigger job: it runs the whole organization, not just donor records. If donor tracking is all you need, Little Green Light costs much less. Here's how the two compare, using Little Green Light's own pricing page.

HelpOne is $299/mo or $2,990/yr with every module. Little Green Light starts at $45/mo for up to 2,500 constituents. See pricing.

Who each one is best for

Choose HelpOne if…

You need more than a donor database: volunteers, events, HR, board and finance in one login, at one price that doesn't change with your list size.

Choose Little Green Light if…

You mainly need an affordable donor database, your list is small, and you want unlimited users with no contract. You can handle volunteers, HR and accounting in other tools.

Where Little Green Light is stronger

  • Price. $45 to $135 a month covers up to 50,000 constituents. That is well below HelpOne for a donor database alone.
  • Simple. One plan, every feature in every account, unlimited users, no contract and no setup or cancellation fees.
  • Donor tools in every account. Mailings, acknowledgments, lists, scheduled reports and bulk data clean-up.
  • Support. Office hours Monday to Friday, 9am to 7pm Eastern, email support and live Q&A webinars twice a week.

If you only need to track donors and gifts on a small budget, Little Green Light is a strong choice.

Where HelpOne is different

  • More than donors. Volunteer shifts and sign-up, events and tickets, HR, training, board and finance share one login and one record per person.
  • One price. $299 a month or $2,990 a year covers every module. It doesn't change as your list grows.
  • Nonprofit finances. Financial statements with fund accounting, Excel and PDF export, and an auto-filled Form 990 and CHAR500. It doesn't replace your CPA.
  • Fewer tools. Replace the volunteer, e-sign, HR and event tools you may be paying for next to your donor database.

Typical cost of separate tools for volunteers, HR, e-sign, events and a donor database: about $1,500 a month on average (our estimate; yours will vary).

Questions

Is HelpOne cheaper than Little Green Light?

No. For donor tracking alone, Little Green Light costs much less: it starts at $45/month for up to 2,500 constituents, and $135/month for up to 50,000. HelpOne is $299/month or $2,990/year, and covers volunteers, events, HR, finances and your board as well. HelpOne can cost less overall if you pay for several other tools today. Typical separate-tool cost is about $1,500 a month on average.

Who is Little Green Light best for?

Small and growing nonprofits that mainly need an affordable donor database. It's simple, has unlimited users, and doesn't lock you into a contract.

Does HelpOne do everything Little Green Light does for donors?

Not everything. HelpOne covers donor records, giving history, pledges, auctions, sponsorships, giving statements, duplicate merge and Mailchimp sync. Little Green Light is a focused donor database, and some teams prefer how simple it is.

Can I move my data from Little Green Light to HelpOne?

Yes. Little Green Light says you can export your data, with no fee. Export your constituents and gifts, start the free trial, and import them. Migration help is available. NYCON members get free migration.

Is there a contract?

Not with HelpOne. No contracts. Cancel anytime in two clicks, and take your data with you. Little Green Light also has no contracts.

Can I try HelpOne first?

Yes. 14 days, every module, no card. When the trial ends, your data stays saved and read-only until you pick a plan.

RUNNING MEMBERSHIP USUALLY MEANS

Chasing renewals in one tool and everything else in five more.

Membership work is simple until you try to keep it all straight. Who's due this month? Did that check get recorded? Is the directory up to date? Did the member price work at the gala? Most groups answer those questions from different places.

RENEWALS

Reminders that confuse people, or that someone has to remember to send.

DUES

Card payments in one place, checks and cash in a notebook, and nothing matching the books.

MEMBER DIRECTORY

Out of date, hard to search, and unclear about who can see what.

MEMBER PRICES ON EVENTS

Discount codes passed around by email, or someone checking a list at the door.

LOTS OF SEPARATE TOOLS

A membership tool, a donor list, an event site and an accounting system that don't talk to each other. HelpOne puts members next to everything else you already run.

How it works

Set up your levels once. HelpOne handles the reminders, the statuses and the records.

🏷️

Levels that fit how you work

Individual, household, student, sustaining, whatever you offer. Renew on the join date or on a set date like January 1. Calendar-year levels can charge only for the months left when someone joins mid-year.

🏠

Households on one renewal

A household level covers the number of people you set. Everyone shares one renewal date, and the main member can add or remove people from their own member page.

📝

Your own join page

People pick a level, add their details and join. You can review applications for any level before they start, and get a notice when someone new joins.

💳

Pay the way you already take payments

If you've connected your card account in HelpOne, members pay online and HelpOne adds no fee for using your own processor. If not, they get an invoice and you record the check or cash.

🔔

Renewal reminders on your schedule

Out of the box, reminders go 30 days before, 7 days before, on the due day and 7 days after. Change those days any time. Each reminder goes once, never twice.

🔗

One click to renew, no password

Every reminder has the member's private renew link. After the due date they get a grace period you set, and then they show as Lapsed.

👥

Plain words for every status

Pending, Active, Grace, Lapsed, Cancelled. Each member shows their level, renewal date and how many days are left. Filter to anyone due in the next 30 days in one click.

📇

Lapsed members stay on file

Nobody has to be archived to keep the bill down. Lapsed members are just a filter, and you can still reach them.

🧾

Checks, cash and part payments

Record a check, cash or a bank transfer against any invoice, with the check number. Record part of it, and the invoice shows what's paid and what's left.

📚

Dues in the books, already

Every dues payment posts to the books as Membership Dues, so your finance reports include it with no re-typing. Move someone to a new level mid-term and they get credit for the unused part.

Members get a private link by email. No account, no password to forget. They see their status and renewal date, renew in one click, and find their invoices and receipts.

Set a member price on any ticket type. Current members, including anyone in their grace period, get it at checkout when they use the email on their membership. No codes to hand out.

Import

Bring your members over from a CSV

It works with a Wild Apricot export or any spreadsheet with the same columns.

  1. Export from Wild Apricot. Go to Contacts, then Export. Pick CSV and all fields.
  2. Open Membership Services in HelpOne. Go to Import & export and choose the file.
  3. Check the preview. HelpOne tells you how many are new, how many are already here and will be updated, and how many will be skipped, before anything changes.
  4. Import. Levels you don't have yet are added for you. Set their prices under Levels before renewals go out.

Columns HelpOne reads: User ID, First name, Last name, Email, Phone, Organization, City, Membership level, Membership status, Member since and Renewal due. Wild Apricot statuses are mapped to HelpOne's. Need your data back out? Download every member as a CSV any time.

Questions

Is Memberships an extra cost?

No. Memberships is part of the one HelpOne plan, with every other module. There are no per-member or per-contact fees. See pricing.

Do members need a password?

No. Members get a private link to their member page by email. The link is good for 60 days, and asking for a new one replaces the old one. Anyone can ask for their link from the join page with their email.

Can a household or family share one membership?

Yes. Set how many people a level covers. Everyone shares one renewal date, and when the main member renews, everyone on the membership renews. The main member can add or remove people from their own member page.

Can we renew everyone on January 1?

Yes. A level can renew on the join date or on a set date. Calendar-year levels can charge only for the months left when someone joins mid-year.

Can we approve people before they become members?

Yes. Turn on review for any level, and new applications wait for you as Pending.

We still get a lot of checks. Is that OK?

Yes. Record a check, cash or bank transfer against any invoice, with the check number. You can record part of an invoice, and it shows the balance.

What happens when someone doesn't renew?

After the due date they get a grace period you set (30 days to start). Then they show as Lapsed. They stay on file, and you can still reach them.

Do lapsed members or event guests raise our price?

No. HelpOne isn't priced by contacts, so you never need to archive anyone to save money.

How do member prices on events work?

Set a member price on a ticket type. When a current member buys a ticket with the email on their membership, they get the member price at checkout.

Can one person hold two memberships at the same time?

Not yet. Each person has one membership. Members can still be in as many groups and lists as you like in HelpOne.

Will importing from Wild Apricot make duplicates?

No. The import matches on email, then on the Wild Apricot User ID. Run it twice and it updates the same people.

Can we get our data out?

Yes. Download every member as a CSV any time, from Import & export. No contracts, and you can cancel any time.

What's new for events

Guests can already change or cancel on their own. Door check-in, RSVPs and pledges are in the same event. Here's what just landed.

🎫

Type the number, skip the tapping

Guests shouldn't have to tap plus ten times to buy a table. They type the ticket quantity, and the total bar no longer covers the screen on phones.

📝

A You're booking recap

Before they confirm, guests see a You're booking recap with the event, the time, the address and what they chose.

⏳

Sold out? Join the waitlist

A sold-out event shouldn't turn people away at the ticket page. Guests join the waitlist right there.

📧

The waitlist can email itself

You shouldn't have to email the waitlist by hand when a seat opens. Turn it on for that event. It starts off. When seats open, HelpOne emails only as many people as fit, oldest first, and holds those seats for 48 hours.

🏆

A leaderboard on the give page

People and Teams views, so fundraisers can see how they're doing. Fundraisers with $0 raised stay hidden.

🏅

Teams and milestone badges

Each fundraiser's page shows their team and five milestone badges.

🤝

Pick a team when you start

When someone starts their own fundraiser, they can pick a team.

🗺️

The guest pass has the address

The pass now shows the full address, a Directions button, and Add to calendar.

At the door and in checkout

A ticket sold a second ago should show at the door. Add-ons, waivers and member prices belong in the same checkout.

🚪

The door never misses a ticket

A ticket sold a second ago shows at the door right away. A second scan warns Already here, instead of checking someone in twice.

🎟️

Sell at the door from the same ticket count

Sell at the door from the same ticket count, by card through the organization's own account, cash or comp.

🎁

Add-ons, in checkout and at the door

Parking, raffle bundles, drink tickets and program ads. They show in checkout, on the recap and at the door.

✍️

Sign the waiver with a finger

Buyers sign the waiver and photo release with a finger in checkout. The door flags anyone who hasn't signed, and staff can get it signed on the spot.

🏷️

Member prices, or join on the same order

Members are recognized by email and get member prices. Everyone else sees Members save $X, join now, and can join on the same order.
